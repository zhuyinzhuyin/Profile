User Flow & Prototyping

Connect every step of creation

I developed high-fidelity screens, reusable components and interactive prototypes to explain the experience and review design decisions with stakeholders and engineers.

DEVELOPMENT SUPPORT

Development Support

Carry the design through to launch

I reviewed staging and production to check design fidelity, filed and tracked UI/UX issues in GitHub, and worked with engineers to resolve them. Ongoing feedback during sprints helped keep the implementation aligned with the intended experience.

Data Analytics

Understand where creators get stuck

I monitored user behavior with Amplitude, OpenReplay and Google Analytics to understand drop-off points, feature adoption and interface engagement. These reports helped identify questions to investigate and opportunities to improve the experience.
