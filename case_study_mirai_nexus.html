The Appraisal Workspace

Keeping the Spreadsheet Grid

Most of the appraisal team knew Excel well and had little experience with other tools. I kept the familiar spreadsheet layout for scanning and comparing leads, then added filters and bulk actions so staff could handle routine appraisal work in the same view.

Complex Appraisal Calculations Automated In One Place

Mirai Nexus calculates pickup costs and estimated gross profit from the case details. Staff can review the figures and confirm a price in the same workspace.

10+ Built-in Automations

  • Model & serial number correction
  • Production year matching
  • Transport costs
  • Remove duplicate leads
  • Appraisal value estimates
  • Gross profit
  • Customer outreach & follow-up

Keep Appraisal Data Up to Date

Staff can update the reference data and rules behind appraisal calculations in one workspace. They can review and maintain historical records there as well.

Staff Roles and Permissions

I designed the User Access workspace so administrators can review each staff member’s permissions and update them by module. For example, they can give one account access to both Purchase Management and Inventory.

Guided by 16 years of design experience.
